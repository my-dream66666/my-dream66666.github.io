题目描述

You are given a positive integer NNN .
We call a non-empty sequence of positive integers AAA a good sequence if it satisfies all of the following conditions:

  • All elements of AAA are distinct.
  • The product of all elements of AAA equals NNN .

The score of a sequence is defined as the sum of all elements of the sequence.
Find the sum, modulo 998244353998244353998244353 , of the scores of all good sequences.
